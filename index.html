Our Nigerian Farm Story

Founded in 2025, we are committed to sustainable and ethical pig farming right here in Nigeria. Our mission is to provide high-quality protein to the local market while supporting the national agricultural economy.

Ethical Farming: Our pigs are raised in spacious, clean pens and fed a balanced diet of locally sourced, organic feed, reducing cost and supporting local businesses.

Economic Impact: By focusing on low-input, high-yield methods, we ensure our products remain affordable for Nigerian families and businesses.

Location: Located in kudendan, we are strategically positioned to serve clients across the Nigeria.
